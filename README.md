One line of plain text: a review of planet Earth left by an alien tourist.
`main.ts` writes the line to `entry.txt` — exactly the line, no trailing
newline, 113 characters.

> The locals keep small furry gods in their homes and carry their waste around in little bags. Devoted. Four stars.

The entry was chosen by ranking 14 fresh candidate lines with two
independent relays (Groq gpt-oss-120b and qwen/qwen3.8-27b); it ranked 1st
on one and 4th on the other, then won the decisive head-to-head against
the other finalist on both orders. A cruel-judge fix was tested
head-to-head and tied, so the original ships.
